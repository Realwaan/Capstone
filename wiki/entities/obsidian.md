# 🧩 Entity: Obsidian

## 📖 Overview
**Obsidian** is a local-first, extensible markdown knowledge base application. In the context of the [[persistent-compounding-wiki|LLM Wiki]], Obsidian acts as the **Integrated Development Environment (IDE)** through which the human user explores the knowledge graph, while the LLM acts as the autonomous programmer continuously editing the codebase.

---

## ⚙️ Recommended Wiki Extensions & Settings

1. **Graph View**: Visualizes interconnected clusters, entity hubs, and isolated orphan nodes.
2. **Dataview Plugin**: Enables SQL-like querying over YAML frontmatter across all wiki documents.
3. **Marp Slides**: Renders markdown presentation slide decks directly from wiki files.
4. **Web Clipper**: Quickly captures web articles directly into `wiki/raw/` with local image asset storage (`wiki/raw/assets/`).
