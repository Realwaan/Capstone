# 🧩 Entity: Vannevar Bush

## 📖 Biography & Significance
**Vannevar Bush** (1890–1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator who headed the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development during World War II.

In his landmark 1945 essay *"As We May Think"* (published in *The Atlantic Monthly*), Bush proposed the concept of the **Memex** — an electro-mechanical device in which an individual stores books, records, and communications, and which is mechanized so that it may be consulted with exceeding speed and flexibility via [[associative-trails|associative trails]].

---

## 🏛️ Influence on Modern Second Brains
Bush foresaw the cognitive limitation of index-based file hierarchies, advocating for associative linking. Modern systems like [[obsidian|Obsidian]] combined with an [[persistent-compounding-wiki|LLM Wiki agent]] realize Bush's vision by automating the creation and maintenance of associative trails.
